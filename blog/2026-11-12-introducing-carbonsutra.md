# Introducing CarbonSutra

Welcome to the CarbonSutra blog.

CarbonSutra provides developers, businesses, and researchers with access to environmental intelligence through a unified collection of APIs and tools.

## What is CarbonSutra?

CarbonSutra makes environmental data more accessible through developer-friendly APIs, integrations, and intelligent solutions.
