# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 29, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 29, 2026  

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **CarbonSutra** (accessible at [https://carbonsutra.com](https://carbonsutra.com)), an enterprise-grade carbon emission estimation engine operated by **Contactous** ("Company", "we", "our", or "us"), a registered entity in Singapore. 

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how CarbonSutra collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you interact with our APIs, tools, and services.

---

## 2. Scope & Methods of Access

CarbonSutra provides carbon emission calculations exclusively through three distinct access channels. The personal data collected is minimal and strictly limited to what is necessary to deliver our services.

### Access Channel 1: Third-Party API Marketplaces & External Portals
When developers or users access CarbonSutra APIs via third-party marketplaces (e.g., RapidAPI, Postman Public Workspace, API.Market, ZYLA) or external developer portals:
* **Data Collected:** Information collected during signup on third-party platforms is governed by the respective marketplace's privacy policy. CarbonSutra only processes authentication headers (API keys or tokens) and operational calculation parameters required to execute API requests.

### Access Channel 2: CarbonSutra Google Workspace Add-on
When users install and authorize the CarbonSutra Google Sheets Add-on via the Google Workspace Marketplace:
* **Data Collected:** We collect only your **Google email address** and **full name**.
* **Purpose:** This information is used exclusively to generate and transmit your unique CarbonSutra API key and facilitate service authorization.
* **Data Protection & Non-Disclosure:** We do **not** sell, share, transfer, or disclose your Google email address, name, or any associated profile data to any third party, advertiser, or external service.

### Access Channel 3: CarbonSutra API Playground
When users test endpoints interactively on our website ([https://carbonsutra.com/api-playground](https://carbonsutra.com/api-playground)):
* **Data Collected:** We collect your **email address**.
* **Purpose:** This email address is used solely to generate and send you an immediate Authorization Code / API key to run live API test calls.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the minimal personal data collected strictly for the following operational purposes:
1. To generate, deliver, and manage your API authentication keys and tokens.
2. To authenticate your identity across our services and measure usage limits (e.g., free tier allowances).
3. To communicate critical service updates, technical notices, or security alerts.
4. To respond to customer support inquiries and technical issues.

We do **not** use your personal information for marketing profiling, automated decision-making, advertising targeting, or unsolicited promotional outreach.

---

## 4. Google API Services User Data & Limited Use Policy

CarbonSutra's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the **[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services/user-data-policy)**, including the **Limited Use requirements**.

Specifically regarding Google Add-on Users:
* **No Third-Party Transfer:** We do not share, disclose, or transfer any Google user data (including names and email addresses) to third parties.
* **No Advertising Use:** Human or automated reading of Google user data for advertising purposes is strictly prohibited. Your Google user data is never used to serve targeted or general advertisements.
* **No AI Training:** Google user data is never used to train, retrain, or improve generalized Artificial Intelligence (AI) or Machine Learning (ML) models.
* **Minimal Scope Request:** The Google Add-on requests only the minimum OAuth permissions required to run spreadsheet functions (`spreadsheets.currentonly`) and identify your account email for API key delivery.

## 5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We maintain a strict zero-sale policy. We do **not** sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with any external commercial third parties. 

We will only disclose information if required to do so by applicable law, legal process, court order, or governmental request from Singapore or relevant international jurisdictions.

---

## 6. Data Security & Encryption

We take data security very seriously. All communications to and from CarbonSutra—including API calls, website traffic, API Playground submissions, and Google Add-on interactions—are strictly encrypted in transit using **SSL/TLS (Secure Sockets Layer / Transport Layer Security)** over HTTPS.

We enforce administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

---

## 7. Data Retention and Inactivity Purge

We retain your personal data (name and email address tied to API keys) only as long as you remain an active user of CarbonSutra services.

* **6-Month Inactivity Rule:** If an account or user remains inactive and stops using CarbonSutra services for a continuous period of **6 months**, your personal data and associated API keys are automatically purged and removed from our active systems.
* **Transactional Anonymity:** Calculation logs, request payloads, and aggregated emission benchmarks are retained in anonymized, non-personally-identifiable formats for operational stability and auditing metrics.
