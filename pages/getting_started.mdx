## 1. Risk-Free Immediate Test

Want to see how CarbonSutra handles your specific emission parameters without setting up payment details? You can run live queries directly from your browser in seconds[cite: 4].

### How it works:

1. Head over to our **[API Playground](https://carbonsutra.com/api-playground)**.
2. Enter your email to generate an **Authorization Code** (it arrives instantly in your inbox—be sure to check your spam folder just in case!).
3. In the Playground, select your desired endpoint (e.g., Business Flights, Vehicle Models, Hotel Stays, Freight, or Electricity).
4. Paste your code into the **Authorization** tab, configure your test inputs in the **Params** tab, and hit **Send**!

## 3. Starting with Google Sheets

Not writing application code today? No problem! Accessing CarbonSutra via Google Sheets is actually our **most popular route** for non-technical users, accountants, and ESG leads managing bulk data.

* **Pre-Built Custom Functions:** Execute custom spreadsheet formulas like `=CO2FLIGHT()`, `=CO2HOTEL()`, `=CO2VEHICLEMODEL()`, `=CO2ELECTRICITY()`, and `=CO2FREIGHT()` straight inside your cells.
* **Open Source Source Code:** We provide 9 fully commented, ready-to-use Google Apps Script functions so you can inspect the code or deploy it instantly.

👉 **[Explore 9 Sample Functions & Google Sheets Setup Guide](https://carbonsutra.com/google-sheet-functions)**
