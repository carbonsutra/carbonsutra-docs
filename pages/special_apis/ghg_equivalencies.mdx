# GHG Equivalencies

This API will be released in September '26. It is a Greenhouse Gas Equivalencies Calculator which convert emissions into concrete terms you can understand — such as the annual CO2 emissions of cars, households, and power plants.

The API will helps you translate abstract measurements into concrete terms you can understand, such as the annual emissions from cars, households, or power plants. It will be useful in communicating your greenhouse gas reduction strategy, reduction targets, or other initiatives aimed at reducing greenhouse gas emissions.
