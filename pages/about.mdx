## 🎯 Our Mission: Eliminating the "Black Box" in Carbon Accounting

In an era of mandatory disclosure frameworks (such as CSRD, ISSB, and SEC climate rules), a carbon footprint estimate is only as good as the methodology supporting it. Most carbon tools act as "black boxes"—returning raw totals without proof.

We built CarbonSutra around **total methodological transparency**:

* **Dynamic API Explanations:** Every calculation payload automatically appends a clear, step-by-step, human-readable breakdown detailing source standards, Great-Circle distance equations, and unit conversion logic directly in the JSON response.

* **Audit-Proof Standards:** Powered by verified baselines including UK DEFRA 2026 conversion factors, Cornell CHSB hotel indices, ICAO travel baselines, and regional electricity grids.

* **Zero-Storage Data Clustering:** Group, tag, and retrieve calculation sets using custom labels without needing to store or manage raw emission state on your end.

## 📈 Proven Track Record & Roadmap

Operational since 2022, CarbonSutra has processed **millions of calculations** for independent developers, non-profits, and global enterprise platforms running on SAP and Oracle backends.

Driven directly by customer demand, our active development roadmap includes:

* **AI-Native Operations:** Native integration with Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers, enabling LLMs and AI agents to execute automated, natural language carbon audits.


* **Open Data Standards:** Native export support for the OpenCCF (Open Corporate Climate Data) schema for frictionless enterprise ERP interoperability.
* **Digital Decarbonization:** Specialized algorithms for Scope 3.1 spend-based procurement and cloud/AI workload footprinting.
